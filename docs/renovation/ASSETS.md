# Asset provenance audit

Ground rule 3: ship nothing of ppy's, and only assets that are clearly redistributable. This file records where every shipped asset comes from and what happens to it. Anything marked **replace** must be gone before the first public release (workstream H checks this list).

- Audit started 2026-10-04 on the `assets/` tree inherited from neomod `0fedcafb`.
- The comparison reference was ppy/osu-resources, cloned into `ref/` for comparison only and never shipped. Its assets are CC-BY-NC 4.0.

## Built-in default skin (`assets/materials/default/`, 260 files)

- neomod credits it to community skins: "- Jaret - Mish Mosh", "Talrava XIV", "Phaze", "Aesthetic 1.3", "xXx_SampleSkin_xXx v1.3", "Stepmania", "Murakumo", "Burning_Crow V2", plus Icomoon and Freepik.
- None of these licences permit redistribution explicitly, and community skins often reuse osu! default elements.
- **Hash comparison against osu-resources:**
  - 8 files are byte-identical: `drum-sliderwhistle.wav`, `normal-sliderwhistle.wav`, `soft-hitclap2.wav`, `soft-hitfinish2.wav`, `soft-hitwhistle.wav`, `soft-sliderslide.wav`, `soft-sliderslide2.wav`, `soft-sliderwhistle.wav`. All of them equal `Samples/UI/noclick-select.wav`, which is an **empty, header-only WAV** (0 frames) with no creative content. Regenerate them anyway, to be clean.
  - 6 audio files share names with osu-resources samples but differ in content: `combobreak.mp3`, `failsound.mp3`, `sectionfail.mp3`, `sectionpass.mp3`, `drum-sliderslide.wav`, `normal-sliderslide.wav`. They may be re-encodes of osu! sounds; they need listening checks or replacement.
  - No PNG is byte-identical, but resized or recoloured derivatives of osu! default elements can't be ruled out by hashing.
- **Verdict: replace the whole default skin** with original or OFL/CC0/CC-BY work, as part of workstream F. The new look is the default skin, so this was always the plan.
  - Until then the inherited skin stays in development builds.
  - It must not ship in a public release.

## Checklist for adding any asset

1. Original work: record the generator script or source file.
2. Third-party: record the source URL, author and licence, and copy the licence text next to the asset or into `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES`.
3. Never add anything from osu-resources, the osu! client or website, Torus, or the osu! logo or cookie.
