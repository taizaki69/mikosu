## Third-party libraries

Every component mikosu ships, links or includes is listed with its exact licence text in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt` (generated by `tools/release/third_party_notices.py` from the sources a build uses). The in-game Licenses tab shows neomod's `assets/misc/licenses.txt`. Among them:
- SDL3, FreeType, libpng, libjpeg-turbo, zlib-ng, bzip2, brotli, fmt, spdlog, GLM, simdutf, ctre, libarchive, nsync, mimalloc, curl, OpenSSL, nghttp2, rapidjson, discord-rpc
- SoLoud (neoloud fork), Signalsmith Stretch, mpg123, FFmpeg (built with its GPL parts, so GPL-2.0-or-later), libiconv, libacl
- stb_image, glad

**BASS** (un4seen) is proprietary and free for non-commercial use only. Whether mikosu ships it or defaults to the open-source SoLoud backend is decided before release.
